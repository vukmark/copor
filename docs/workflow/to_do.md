### **The To-Do Hub: Organizing Your Tasks**

The **To-Do** page is your central space for managing, tracking, and organizing all your tasks efficiently. It provides a clear overview of your responsibilities, complete with details on priority, progress, and deadlines. To help you quickly find what you're looking for, the page includes powerful search, filter, and sort functionalities.

---

#### **Private To-Dos**

Every user can create tasks tailored to their specific needs:

**Your Personal Task List**

These tasks are completely personal and visible only to you. They are perfect for managing individual assignments, reminders, and goals.

-   **Flexible Tagging:** Organize your private to-dos using two types of tags:
    -   **Admin Tags:** Pre-defined tags set up by system administrators.
    -   **Private Tags:** Custom tags that you can create and color-code yourself.

-   **Customizable & Accessible Colors:** The colors for your private tags are drawn from the **Color Set** you select in your "My Account" preferences. This unique feature allows you to choose a palette that is optimized for your specific color vision needs, ensuring your tags are always clear and distinguishable.
